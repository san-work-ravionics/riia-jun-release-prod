Jarvis

One conversation from your goal to a tracked portfolio. Jarvis takes the expected return from What I Need, has RITA pick the instruments that meet it, and tracks and protects them with FnO.

What Jarvis will do

  1. Read your goal and horizon from What I Need, per location (Europe, India, US).
  2. Pick the location's instruments with the lowest risk whose history meets the goal.
  3. Show your portfolio by geography: P&L over time, return against the target, and each instrument's chart.
  4. Show entry, exit and stop loss from each engine (RITA model, FnO levels, ASTA) with the reasons, and how each engine performs.
  5. Raise the goal and re-select when a stop loss is hit, within the agreed guard.
